# Code of Conduct

This project serves children, teens, teachers, and families. We hold ourselves to a high standard.

- Be kind, patient, and respectful. Assume good intent; critique work, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Never post or request personal information about children, and never contribute content that sexualizes, targets, or endangers minors.
- Do not submit agents designed to deceive, manipulate, collect personal data, or help students cheat.
- Report concerns to the maintainers (see the STARS contact on the landing page). Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation for violations.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
